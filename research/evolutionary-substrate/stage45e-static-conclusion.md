# Stage 4.5E static substrate conclusion

This conclusion is construction and compiler evidence only. It makes no
profitability, activation, or market-runtime claim.

- The frozen five-host one-step language has 2,594 raw plans; 2,381 are
  compiler-admitted and full-pair accepted. The other 213 are concrete
  compiler exclusions: 210 lack a required compiled guard kind and three
  violate the duplicate break-even invariant.
- Nominal diversity is partly redundant: 2,381 plans become 2,241 distinct
  child side-programs and executable semantics. The 4,000 conditional draws
  become 1,225 plans but 1,196 executable outcomes.
- Resource substitution hides the broadest internal variation: 361 rows carry
  only 221 executable outcomes. By contrast the sampled hold, protection, and
  typed-grammar vocabularies are repeatedly covered almost completely while
  the 1,851-plan indicator-learning envelope reaches 767 plans (41.4%).
- Exact delta taxonomy shows 198 scalar/parameter changes, 412
  hold/protection-only changes, 328 existing-guard local changes, 361 existing
  resource-object changes, 674 resource-plus-binding changes, 280 event route
  adds/removes, 98 single structural-region changes, and 30 coherent
  multi-object motifs. Leaf-path count alone is not a blast-radius measure.
- The bounded compile-only round trip restored eight of ten representatives in
  one admitted step, preserving the side program, opposite side, and pair
  executable semantic. A typed-guard replacement and one topology rewire
  exposed no one-step inverse; this is not an impossibility result.
- Entry event gating is not shown unavailable. The 210 failed entry inserts
  specifically fail a missing compiled guard kind, rather than a demonstrated
  cap, budget, binding, or guard-depth rule.
- All ten compatible crossover cases preserve topology semantic identity while
  replacing hub edges; their children have distinct executable semantics from
  both parents. Treat them as semantically equivalent hub replacements, not
  demonstrated useful grafts.

The current substrate is **statically locally navigable** for the restored
representatives, **statically redundant/oversampled** in small conditional
families and known aliases, **host/context dependent** for coverage and
admission, and **requires synthetic runtime evidence** for activation,
behavioral distance, or any economic conclusion.
