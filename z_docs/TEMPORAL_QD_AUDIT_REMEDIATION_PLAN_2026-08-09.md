# Temporal QD audit remediation plan

Date: 2026-08-09

Status: implemented and locally admitted on 2026-08-09; no new economic campaign
authorized. See `TEMPORAL_QD_AUDIT_REMEDIATION_CHECKPOINT_2026-08-09.md` and the
content-bound capacity receipt for verification and remaining boundaries.

## Outcome sought

The completed `4,000 -> 1,024 x 5` campaign proved that the system can construct,
evaluate, restart, and scrutinize thousands of immutable bidirectional temporal
programs. It did not yet prove that the evolutionary search is learning over
canonical evidence or exploring a sufficiently broad strategy language.

The next campaign is admitted only when:

1. every result is bound to one canonical observation stream, candidate, program,
   panel, and half-open window;
2. risk denominators are finite, practical, and immutable;
3. archive cells describe their claimed behavior;
4. realized behavior, direction, branch liveness, and operator contribution are
   observable;
5. configured offspring and immigrant allocation is explicit rather than an
   accidental consequence of sparse parents;
6. the generator can create and recombine materially different evidence, event,
   temporal, management, and graph motifs rather than only decorate three roots.

## Priority 0: correctness and evidence admission

### Canonical active-file evidence

- Readers consume only the content-bound active-file set admitted by the cache
  authority; stale sibling parquet files never participate in a replay.
- Observation count, first/last timestamp, frozen bounds, and ordered stream hash
  are recorded and checked at every admission boundary.
- Cache replacement removes or isolates obsolete artifacts and fails closed on
  missing, unlisted, or conflicting files.

### Candidate, program, panel, and window rebinding

- Every embedded result record is rebound to its owning candidate identity,
  authored/raw and normalized profile identities, resolved program, panel/window
  IDs, and exact half-open bounds.
- Panel construction and cumulative reduction both repeat the checks.
- Sidecar admission recomputes canonical evidence identity when a predeclared
  context exists; rotating panel-scoped identities retain their separate contract.

### Risk-unit safety

- Dynamically resolved stops below a documented practical minimum fail closed.
- Position rehydration proves that entry price, initial stop price, and immutable
  stop-loss percent describe the same initial risk.
- Break-even, tightening, and trailing never change the denominator used for R.
- Conservative cost arithmetic, next-open execution, and stop-first ambiguous-bar
  behavior remain unchanged.

### Indicator orientation

The generic lookback helper currently operates on the scoring engine's
newest-first arrays. Its forward slice is therefore historical in that coordinate
system; it must not be blindly reversed. The contract becomes explicit and gains
direct orientation plus prefix/append-invariance tests. Any chronological caller
must adapt deliberately or fail admission.

## Priority 1: archive and selection truth

### QD descriptor repair

Repair and version the axes that affected cell assignment:

- operator families must read the persisted lineage shape;
- root construction records do not count as mutation depth;
- management counts include real management actions and exclude ordinary entry
  and exit;
- graph-size bins partition the actually observed composite graph range.

Python and Rust both validate the seven-coordinate cell geometry, exact cell ID,
globally unique cells/candidates, and member-to-cell consistency. Existing frozen
archives remain readable as historical authorities but are never silently
reinterpreted. A deterministic rebuild/diff explains how membership changes.

### Selection universes and terminology

- `new proposal`, `archive incumbent`, `mechanically valid`, `terminal complete`,
  `quality`, `frontier`, `breeding eligible`, and `promoted` remain distinct fields.
- Post-campaign scrutiny uses an immutable, deduplicated union of the final archive
  and the final new-proposal cohort so a carried incumbent cannot disappear.
- Terminal completion remains fail closed; there is no invented liquidation.

### Explicit reproduction

- The frozen authority declares desired offspring and immigrant counts.
- The artifact records scheduled, attempted, valid, accepted, and evaluated counts
  by origin and reason.
- Parent sampling with replacement or a separately named reservoir prevents sparse
  parent supply from silently changing the experiment into immigrant sampling.
- A minimum immigrant floor remains, and unsupported parents are not forced merely
  to meet a nominal ratio.

## Priority 2: realized behavior and direction

Add content-bound observability before widening the language:

- per-side active-window fraction, trades, gross/net/cost R, drawdown, exposure,
  holding, terminal status, and conflict abstentions;
- per-transition/guard/event/action evaluate, true/false, fire, schedule, apply,
  reject, and cancel counts by direction and window;
- parent/operator/authored-change/executed-change/retention attribution;
- a realized behavior identity over entries, effects, fired transitions, direction,
  holding, and economics, separate from exact genotype/program identity;
- behavior-family clustering and scrutiny deduplication that can identify passenger
  mutations without deleting legitimate genotype diversity.

Selection retains separately labeled lanes for balanced bidirectional behavior and
direction specialists. One side may not silently subsidize an inactive or harmful
other side. This does not delete shorts, require both sides to be profitable, or
change symmetric execution arithmetic.

## Priority 3: a richer search substrate

The target is not unconstrained program synthesis. It is a large set of small,
typed, composable grains that can be inserted, removed, rewired, crossed, measured,
and rejected deterministically.

### Selected compiler seam

New campaigns use an AutoResearch-owned `EvolvableModuleGenomeV1` as the mutable
genotype. It is deterministically compiled into the existing Dashboard-owned v2
one-direction profile and then combined through the admitted v3 bidirectional
compiler. The Dashboard indicator, observation, graph, execution, cost, and R
machinery remains the only runtime implementation.

The genome contains a typed resource pool, an entry/setup DAG, prioritized
position-management and exit regions, recovery, a frozen budget contract, and a
compiler-policy identity. Independent management regions are a genotype
abstraction compiled through one shared positioned hub; they do not introduce
concurrent runtime regions, more positions, or more than one pending effect.

Old typed-fragment candidates remain available through a dual-reader codec
registry. New programs use a tagged program kind and new schema identity. No old
candidate, archive, or campaign is upgraded or rehashed in place.

### Fuzzy and indicator grains

- create, split, merge, insert, and remove evidence groups;
- mutate group membership, positive normalized weights, thresholds, and bounded
  lookback/timeframe/indicator parameters;
- insert/remove catalog-authorized indicator instances and capability-compatible
  family substitutions;
- insert/remove catalog-authorized raw directional events;
- retain explicit group/member/entry-route budgets and candidate ownership;
- record group score distribution, threshold margin, member ablation, and operator
  success before raising caps substantially.

### Temporal and graph grains

- count, sequence, absence, time-since-event/condition, condition duration,
  cooldown/re-entry, UTC session, and regime-change facts;
- sealed typed motifs for alternate watch branches, confirmation/rejection,
  re-arm loops, and timeouts;
- graph-level add/remove/rewire operations with state/transition cost accounting,
  reachability checks, dead-branch rejection, and semantic novelty;
- deterministic same-side crossover over compatible typed motif ports, with child
  identity bound to ordered parents and the exact segment map.

### Management topology

Compare the existing serial management cursor with bounded independent or
explicitly prioritized live-position management regions. A new topology must keep
one position, one pending effect, completed-bar decisions, next-open application,
and deterministic conflict resolution. It is adopted only if it activates useful
authored management/exit behavior and reduces dead transitions in a matched A/B.

### Versioning

Every new grammar, operator registry, capability contract, budget, and policy has
an explicit schema/version identity. Old candidates and frozen campaigns remain
byte-readable under their original authorities; they are not upgraded in place.

## Admission ladder

Each slice must pass:

1. direct schema/semantic and negative failure-path tests;
2. deterministic identity, exact replay, and split/restart tests;
3. Python/Rust parity wherever both implementations own the contract;
4. native Dashboard validation and no-market generation admission;
5. targeted behavior/liveness fixtures proving the new grain executes;
6. an old/new oracle comparison for unchanged programs;
7. resource checks that prevent material wall-time or memory regression.

After the correctness slices, run direction/mirror/integrity replays on already
consumed evidence, then a matched serial-versus-independent management A/B, then a
fixed-budget width-versus-lineage diagnostic. Only after those pass should a fresh
canonical `4,096 -> 1,024` two-generation EURUSD checkpoint be considered.

## Stop conditions

Stop immediately for identity, cache, observation geometry, date-bound, source,
reducer, terminal, prefix-invariance, restart, or Python/Rust parity mismatch.

At learning checkpoints, stop or redesign after two rounds with no new/improved
quality cells, fewer than 5-10% new realized behaviors, offspring yield below 1.5x
matched immigrants, no held-panel improvement, complete cost-stress collapse,
rising dead/passenger branches, or one economic family exceeding 50%.

## Explicitly outside this remediation

- multiple simultaneous positions or state-machine invocations;
- pyramiding, scale-in, partial exits, or multi-leg orders;
- portfolio-aware graph state or cross-symbol multi-leg execution;
- arbitrary unbounded hierarchy/parallel regions;
- an enlarged indicator catalog without contribution evidence;
- outer-tail use or a giant campaign justified only as more data.
